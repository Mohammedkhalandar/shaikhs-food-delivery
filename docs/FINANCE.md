# Restaurant financial accounting

- Unpromoted orders snapshot the rule effective at Order.createdAt. Promoted orders bind the immutable rule selected by the authoritative checkout quote inside order creation, keeping credit caps and payable consistent. Restaurant overrides take precedence; a null override inherits the platform rule. Rules and snapshots are append-only. The initial platform rate is explicitly 0% until configured; legacy orders are excluded and counted, never repriced.
- ITEMS_ONLY_V1 uses exact Decimal item subtotal × basis points / 10,000, rounded half-up to two decimals. Item net is subtotal minus commission. Legacy ITEMS_ONLY_V1 snapshots have zero discount. Supported POST_TAX_CREDIT_V1 courtesy credits follow the policy below; other inconsistent totals/allocations fail closed. Delivery fees and recorded tax are excluded and shown separately. This ledger does not determine tax ownership, liability or compliance. Gross customer collections are not platform commission revenue.
- Payable requires delivered orders and verified server evidence: an exact COD receipt, or captured ONLINE payment without review/open refund. ONLINE remains disabled. Disputes and unresolved/partial refund reviews hold balances. Pending and disputed balances remain separate.
- Cancellation or verified full refund reverses the original item net and commission once, transactionally. A refund after settlement creates a negative recovery balance. Every subsequent settlement offsets outstanding negative balances first; a disputed negative balance blocks settlement. Manual signed adjustments require an admin, reason, unique reference and current version; they remain separate from original commission and are not silently removed by refunds.
- Settlements allocate server-calculated credits. References bind the full request; duplicate requests cannot pay twice. Zero-net recovery reconciliation is supported. Reversals append compensating allocations; later dependent settlements must be reversed first. No original evidence is edited/deleted. Restaurant locks, Serializable transactions and database uniqueness protect concurrent actions.
- SIMULATED and MANUAL_RECEIPT settlement allocations/history are isolated in reporting. Disposable development permits only SIMULATED records. A manual receipt elsewhere records an admin's evidence, never initiates or verifies a bank transfer. External payouts remain disabled. Do not promote synthetic development data as actual money.
- Only restaurant owners see their own financial history; staff cannot access it. Admins manage rules, disputes, adjustments and receipts. Search reaches restaurants beyond the initial 200-row list; histories paginate and totals cover the full scoped ledger.

Production tax/discount allocation, bank-transfer reconciliation and external payment/payout onboarding require separate acceptance. Current executed checks and limitations are in TEST_REPORT.md.

Operator review cases preserve actual ledger authority. Case-only simulated credits are excluded from customer paid totals, COD, commission accounts, rider earnings and payout receipts; they never initiate transfers. Refund approval is a deferred request decision until provider onboarding and verified evidence are available. Settlement-dispute reports hold the existing CommissionState; explicit case release records a zero-delta DISPUTE_RESOLVE adjustment under the existing finance lock/version guards and refuses release while another open settlement case exists for that order. Rejection/acknowledgement alone never removes financial holds. Existing financial controls remain authoritative and synchronize cases after their verified changes. Prepared returns reuse the unique original reversal and never create a second stock return, earning, COD receipt or payout.

## Courtesy-credit promotion policy

Promotions are explicit post-tax courtesy credits, not a new tax calculation or a rewrite of menu prices. Existing recorded tax, gross item subtotal and original delivery fee remain visible and unchanged; credit is deducted once from customer total. Only one code can apply. FIXED or PERCENT eligibility covers the item subtotal or delivery fee; percentage credit is rounded half-up once to paise, then capped by eligible base and configured maximum. Total must remain positive. Restaurant-funded credit is additionally capped at the pre-credit restaurant net and reduces restaurant payable; gross commission remains based on the original items. Platform-funded credit is a separately reported platform expense and leaves merchant payable unchanged. Original rider payout is preserved even on delivery-fee offers. This policy does not determine statutory tax obligations or recalculate refund tax allocations.

New immutable PromotionRedemption/Order promotion snapshots bind code, terms/config version, funding, rounded credit and commission-rule ID to each order. Order discount is separate from original line/item prices; OrderCommission uses POST_TAX_CREDIT_V1 for promoted orders. Its broader constraint preserves every legacy ITEMS_ONLY_V1 row with zero discount. Restaurant net+commission equals gross items minus restaurant-funded credit; platform-funded credit is not deducted from merchant books. Reconciled financial reports expose restaurantCredits and platformCredits separately; gross paid collections/COD use the discounted order total. Cancel/full-refund reversals negate the already-snapshotted payable and commission once. No second credit, stock return, earning or payout is inferred.

Promotions are scheduled, versioned and soft-archived. Owners manage only restaurant-funded offers for their own tenant; only ADMIN can authorize platform funding/global scope. Global and per-customer committed-use caps are checked in Serializable checkout with an atomic counter update and unique redemption/order binding. Consumed uses remain consumed after cancellation/refund to prevent repeated budget reuse. Request-bound checkout replay precedes current coupon/availability checks; historical retry quotes require matching owned idempotency key and unchanged normalized intent, use original totals/lines and cannot authorize a new checkout with stale prices. Coupon intent survives a lost-response reload. No external payout/real online payment/message is enabled.
